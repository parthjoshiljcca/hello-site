## Facts and sources

- About 5,029 m (16,500 ft), at the foot of Trishul; frozen much of the year; found in 1942 by Nanda Devi reserve ranger H. K. Madhwal; first feared to be Japanese soldiers, then found to be far older. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roopkund), [Amusing Planet](https://www.amusingplanet.com/2013/01/the-skeleton-lake-of-roopkund-india.html)
- 2004 study: the same short, deep skull fractures from a round object, only on the head and shoulders; weapons, avalanche and landslide ruled out; a severe hailstorm with very large hailstones. [Atlas Obscura via Slate](https://www.slate.com/blogs/atlas_obscura/2013/05/24/an_indian_lake_surrounded_by_hundreds_of_ancient_skeletons_the_surprise.html), [Strange Remains](https://strangeremains.com/2013/09/12/the-mystery-of-skeleton-lake-in-roopkund-india/)
- 2019, Harney et al., *Nature Communications*: DNA from 38 individuals, 23 South Asian-related, 14 eastern Mediterranean-related (closest to present-day Crete and Greece), 1 Southeast Asian-related; South Asian group around 800 CE (more than one event), the rest around 1800 CE; why the Mediterranean group was there is unknown. [Harvard summary](https://meganmichel.scholars.harvard.edu/publications/ancient-dna-skeletons-roopkund-lake-reveals-mediterranean-migrants-india), [ScienceDaily](https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2019/08/190820130926.htm), [Onmanorama](https://onmanorama.com/news/india/2019/08/21/roopkund-lake-uttarakhand-skeletons-himalayas.html)
- The lake lies on the Nanda Devi Raj Jat route; a pilgrimage death may explain some South Asian remains. [Research Matters](https://researchmatters.in/news/where-are-those-skeletons-tumbling-out-mysterious-roopkund-lake)

Kept loose on purpose: the folk tale is told as a tale; the hail size is "cricket-ball", as the sources compare it to a ball of that size (baseball in Western accounts); no total skeleton count beyond "सैकड़ों", since sources disagree.
