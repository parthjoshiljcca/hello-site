ABOUT THE AUTHOR

By day, Prof. Parth D. Joshi teaches young minds how machines think. By night, he writes code while the house sleeps. And somewhere in between, he collects stories — of gods, ghosts, and the places where the two meet.

For twelve years he has been an Assistant Professor at L. J. College of Computer Applications (LJCCA), Ahmedabad, where his students know him as the professor who answers a doubt with a story. He is the founder and mentor of CodeSprint Labs and AiRA Lab.

A believer who codes, he lights a diya every evening, has never once passed a mandir without folding his hands — and still has not googled Nanda Kot 1967.

Hidden Uttarakhand is his latest book.
