If the forest calls your name —
don’t answer.

In October 2026, Professor Parth went to Uttarakhand to relax. He took two friends, one driver, three sweaters, and a notebook for the legends he heard.

He came back with a police statement, a YouTube channel that won’t stop tagging him — and the phone number of a man who wanted something buried under the snow of Nanda Devi badly enough to chase them across an entire state.

In 1965, a nuclear device was lost on that mountain. It has never been found.

From a locked room in Mussoorie to the bells of Chitai, from a lake full of strangers’ bones to a forest that calls your name, Hidden Uttarakhand is a road trip through the untold stories of the Devbhoomi — and a chase that refuses to stay buried.

Everything marked as fact is fact.
Everything marked as legend is legend.
Everything else is a story.
Prof. Parth D. Joshi teaches young minds how machines think, writes code while the house sleeps, and collects stories of gods, ghosts and the places where the two meet. He is the author of Ramayana Reimagined: The Eternal Legacy.
